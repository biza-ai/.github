## 🛠️ Open Source Tooling & Compliance Libraries

We maintain open-source developer tooling to simplify compliance and accounting in the GCC:

- [**`zatca-qr`**](https://github.com/biza-ai/zatca-qr) — Zero-dependency, universal TypeScript encoder and decoder for Saudi ZATCA e-invoicing Phase 1 & Phase 2 QR codes. Works across Browser, Node.js, Deno, Bun, and Edge Workers with accurate Arabic UTF-8 byte calculation.
- [**`saudi-chart-of-accounts`**](https://github.com/biza-ai/saudi-chart-of-accounts) — Standard bilingual (Arabic / English) Chart of Accounts (COA) template conforming to SOCPA, ZATCA 15% VAT, and GOSI rules (available in JSON & CSV).

## 🚀 The BIZA Platform

Looking for a complete, certified ERP system?
- **ZATCA Phase 2 E-Invoicing:** Automated clearance (B2B) and reporting (B2C) with cryptographic onboarding.
- **Finance & Accounting:** Real-time general ledger, automated VAT returns, and multi-currency.
- **Operations & Inventory:** Multi-warehouse tracking, FIFO/Weighted Average valuation, and purchasing.
- **AI Document Capture & Voice:** Scan supplier invoices via camera/PDF and manage operations through voice.
